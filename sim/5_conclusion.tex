\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

\paragraph{Limitations and future work.}
\name allocates tokens according to the relevance scores of the retriever, and cannot recover evidence that the
retriever scores as irrelevant. Moreover, the token value function is shared by all frames, although a frame with
dense on-screen text likely saturates later than a plain scene; predicting it from the content of a frame is a natural
extension. Finally, our multi-turn evaluation covers one VLM.

\paragraph{Conclusion.}
We presented \name, a framework that moves video RAG from selecting frames to allocating tokens. \name allocates one
token budget across frames and transcript segments by iterative water-filling over a learned token value, and carries
the allocation across the turns of a conversation. Across three benchmarks and two VLMs, it improves accuracy at
equal compute and matches the strongest prior method at roughly one third of its compute.
